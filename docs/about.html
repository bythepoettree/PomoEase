About

Focus that keeps you moving.

PomoEase is a focus timer built on a simple idea: the best breaks aren't spent scrolling. You work in focused rounds, and between them PomoEase turns your break into a quick burst of movement — a few reps to get you off your chair, reset, and return sharper for the next round.

It grew out of a frustration familiar to anyone who studies or works at a screen for hours: the standard Pomodoro cycle is great for concentration, but its breaks too often become another window, another feed, another slump. We wanted the pause between rounds to actually restore you — physically as well as mentally.

What we believe

Focus and wellbeing shouldn't compete. A tool that helps you concentrate should also help you look after your body and your attention over the long run. Small, consistent movement beats occasional heroics, and a study session shared with a friend beats one spent alone.

Study on your own, or together. PomoEase lets you run a solo timer or share a live, synced session with a friend — so you can keep pace with each other even when you're apart. A leaderboard is there if a little friendly competition helps you show up.

Free, and built to stay simple

PomoEase runs entirely in your browser — there's nothing to install, and it's free to use. We keep the experience deliberately uncluttered: a timer, your tasks, quick workouts on your breaks, and the people you study with.
