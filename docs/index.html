How PomoEase works

A focus timer that works in rounds: study for a stretch, then take a short break — with optional workouts and friends to keep you going.

The timer
Start / pause Begins your focus round and counts down. Tap again to pause; the button turns into two bars while running.
Reset Sends the current round back to the start without changing your settings.
Workout Turn a break into a quick guided workout — a few exercises instead of sitting still. You still get your break.
Round dots Show how many focus rounds you've finished in this set. After the last one you get a longer break.
Tasks & focus
Add a task List what you're working on and give it an estimate. Each finished round counts towards the task you've picked.
⛶
Focus mode Tapping the timer hides everything but the countdown. Tap anywhere to come back out.
Studying with friends
Friends Share your code to add friends. You'll see who's online, and a dot shows when a friend is studying right now.
Co-work Start a shared room and the timer stays in sync for everyone. Send an Invite, or use Ask to join to knock on a friend's room — they tap once to let you in.
Session chat Inside a room you can send quick messages so a study group can stay in touch without leaving the page.
Top of the screen
Leaderboard The trophy at the top-left opens the global leaderboard — see how your focus minutes and reps rank.
Friends & Co-work The people icon beside the trophy opens your social space — add friends and start a live co-work session with synced timers.
Menu Opens Account, Settings and Support. Settings holds round lengths, theme, language, sound and notifications. Sign in from Account to sync across devices.
Power tools

On your break

Breaks don't have to mean sitting still — pick a short workout sized to your break, or simply rest.

The break screen
Pick a programme Choose a short workout from the list. Each one is sized to fill the time you have on this break.
🎲
Surprise mix & Just rest Let it pick a random set for you, or skip the movement and simply rest the break out.
Back to study The arrow at the top ends the break early and jumps straight back to a focus round.
